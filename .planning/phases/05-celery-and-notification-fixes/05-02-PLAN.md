<objective>
Wrap every confirmed `.delay()` call that fires inside a `transaction.atomic()` block with
`transaction.on_commit(lambda: ...)`, and replace the hardcoded `recipient_id=1` in
`process_recurring_payments` with a dynamic superuser lookup.

Purpose: A `.delay()` inside `atomic()` dispatches the task to Redis before the DB transaction
commits. If the transaction rolls back, the task runs against state that never existed — causing
DoesNotExist errors, duplicate processing, or silent data corruption. This change defers all
dispatches until after commit, eliminating the race.

Output: 6 dispatch sites wrapped with on_commit, hardcoded admin ID replaced, test stubs
written and green.

NOTE: The two send_notification.delay() calls at payments/views.py lines 1178 and 1190
(inside _handle_charge_success) are intentionally NOT touched here — they are replaced by the
new named notification tasks in Plan 03 with on_commit wrapping included in that change.
</objective>
